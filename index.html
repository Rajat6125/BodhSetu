About BodhSetu

Making Public Services Truly Understandable

BodhSetu (बोधसेतु — Bridge of Understanding) transforms complex governmental circulars and welfare schemes into clear, actionable, citizen-first guidance.

Every citizen deserves dignified, transparent access to welfare rights, educational stipends, healthcare subsidies, and civic benefits without administrative friction or confusing legalese.

✓
Simplified Language: Clear terms in your native language, free of jargon.
✓
Instant Eligibility: Answer simple questions to find which welfare schemes you qualify for.
✓
Step-by-Step Guidance: Complete roadmaps from document preparation to application submission.
